About

I'm a mathematician who builds things to understand them. I studied mathematics at Oakland University with a minor in AI, and wrote my honors thesis on a pursuit game on graphs: how many cops it takes to catch a robber when each cop can only reach so far.

Now I'm reading for an MSc in Mathematics and Foundations of Computer Science at Oxford, working through the architecture of Fermat's Last Theorem, and learning Lean 4 on the side.

From 2023 to 2026 I ran a one-on-one tutoring practice in Michigan, from algebra through SAT and ACT prep. Away from the desk: espresso, house and techno, and handmade bracelets.
